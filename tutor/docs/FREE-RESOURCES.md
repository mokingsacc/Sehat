# Free resources we may use

Mo asked us to "use the available free resources out there". Free to watch is not the same as free to copy, so
every source below is sorted by what its licence lets Qalam do. The machine-readable list, with one line of credit
for each source, is [`content/sources.json`](../content/sources.json).

The classes:

- **BUNDLE**: public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA (or a "copy unchanged with credit" grant). We may copy it into
  the app and the hub with credit. With SA, anything we adapt must carry the same licence.
- **BUNDLE-NC**: CC BY-NC or CC BY-NC-SA, or a similar non-commercial grant. Allowed because Qalam is free and
  non-commercial. Never sell it, never put it behind a paywall or ads. ND means no translating or changing.
- **LINK**: free to view, all rights reserved. Link out, or play it in the owner's own player. Never copy.
- **ASK**: worth having, but we need written permission first (drafts exist for Begum, Right to Learn, LEARN and
  SOLAx).
- **AVOID**: the terms forbid our use, or the material is unsafe or unsuitable.

Two rules that catch people out:

- CC BY-SA and CC BY-NC-SA material cannot be mixed into one lesson: their share-alike terms clash. Keep them in
  separate lessons or items.
- Our picture rule (no faces or living beings, see [CONTENT.md](CONTENT.md)) still applies to open material. Many
  open storybooks show children and animals: keep the text and redraw or drop the pictures.

## Use these first

1. **Afghan Children Read readers on Bloom Library (BUNDLE, CC0).** The best find. 209 Pashto and 131 Dari graded
   readers written for the Afghan grade 1-3 reading programme (USAID, Creative Associates, 2021), every one marked
   CC0 in Bloom's catalogue. This is the backbone for early reading, grades 1-3 and adult beginners.
2. **StoryWeaver (BUNDLE, CC BY 4.0).** 122 Pashto, 12 Dari and 268 "Farsi" storybooks (many made with Darakht-e
   Danesh), and 406 Simplified Chinese books for the Chinese track. Translate more from its English stock.
3. **Tanzil Quran text (BUNDLE, unchanged only).** The one Quran text the app uses, shown by verse reference; the
   AI never writes verses.
4. **QuranEnc Dari and Pashto translations (BUNDLE, unchanged only).** Badakhshani (Dari) and Rowwad, Abu Zakaria
   or Sarfaraz (Pashto), under QuranEnc's seven conditions.
5. **Islamic Network recitation audio (BUNDLE-NC).** Reciters license it "for free, non-commercial redistribution";
   the King Fahd Complex may be better once its page can be read (ASK).
6. **PhET simulations released before 29 March 2026 (BUNDLE, CC BY 4.0).** Offline science and maths sims, many in
   Persian (counts not checked); keep the logo and the exact credit line.
7. **Illustrative Mathematics, first edition (BUNDLE, CC BY 4.0).** A complete, well-sequenced maths course for
   grades 1-12 to translate and cut to the Afghan syllabus.
8. **VOA Learning English (BUNDLE, public domain).** Graded English texts with audio and video, free to copy with
   credit (skip AP, Reuters and AFP items).
9. **Khan Academy, including fa.khanacademy.org (BUNDLE-NC).** Maths and science videos; Iranian Persian, so use
   them for older learners and teachers, not as the Dari voice of the app.
10. **Persian and Pashto Wikipedia and Wiktionary (BUNDLE, CC BY-SA 4.0).** Reference text and word lists the AI
    tutor can look things up in offline.
11. **Global Digital Library (BUNDLE, CC BY or CC BY-SA per book).** More Pashto readers and some maths books.
12. **Common Voice Pashto and Piper Persian voices (BUNDLE, CC0 data).** The open start for our own Pashto and Dari
    voice and listening tools.
13. **Begum Academy (ASK; LINK meanwhile).** The complete grade 7-12 Dari and Pashto video course. All rights
    reserved, so link to its YouTube channels until Begum says yes.
14. **Darakht-e Danesh Library (ASK).** The largest Dari and Pashto open library (3,000 to 9,000+ items), reported as
    Creative Commons item by item, but its site blocks us, so ask Right to Learn and read each item's licence.
15. **OpenStax (BUNDLE-NC, with a no-AI clause).** English science and maths for Kankor and university preparation,
    but kept out of the AI tutor's sources because the books forbid LLM ingestion.

## Gaps we must write ourselves

- **Early years:** no Dari or Pashto letter-sound game or phonics sequence exists openly. Build one on the Feed the
  Monster code with our own sounds, and our own songs and counting rhymes. Pictures in open storybooks mostly need
  redrawing for our picture rule.
- **Grades 1 to 6:** Afghan Children Read covers reading to grade 3 only. Nothing open teaches grades 4-6 Dari or
  Pashto, or maths, science, social studies or life skills at any primary grade in Dari or Pashto. Translate
  Illustrative Mathematics and PhET and align them to the MoE syllabus; write science and social studies ourselves.
- **Grades 7 to 9 and 10 to 12:** every complete Dari and Pashto course (Begum, SOLAx, BBC Dars) is all rights
  reserved. Unless Begum or SOLAx say yes, we write lessons, worksheets and question banks, using OpenStax, CK-12 and
  Khan Academy only as English or Persian sources.
- **Kankor:** no open past papers or question banks were found. Write our own practice sets from the syllabus.
- **Adults learning to read:** no open adult literacy primer in Dari or Pashto. Adapt Afghan Children Read texts to
  adult themes (market, health, money, family) and write a short adult primer.
- **English:** no open English course taught from Dari or Pashto. Write the Dari and Pashto instructions and
  explanations ourselves and use VOA Learning English as the reading and listening material.
- **Chinese:** nothing teaches Chinese from Dari or Pashto. Write an HSK 1-level starter (pinyin, tones, about 300
  words) from CC-CEDICT, Wikibooks and StoryWeaver Chinese readers.
- **Quran and seerah:** text, meanings and recitation exist. No open seerah, hadith or Islamic studies text exists in
  Dari or Pashto; these need a qualified Hanafi reviewer to write or approve them, and the AI must never produce
  verses or hadith.
- **Life skills and practical skills:** only Hesperian (with permission) is close. Write money, phone and digital
  safety, first aid and job skills lessons ourselves.
- **Voices:** no open Pashto or Dari voice exists. Record a human narrator for every lesson first; train our own
  voices later from Common Voice.

## How to credit

Every item that came from outside Qalam shows a credit line under it in the app and at the foot of its printed
sheet, and the About screen lists every source with its licence link. The format:

> "{Title}" by {author or owner}, {licence short name}, from {source}. {Changes}.

- **Licence short name** is exactly as written by the owner (CC0, CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY-NC-SA, public
  domain), linked to the licence text when the screen allows.
- **Changes** says what we did: "Translated into Dari by Qalam", "Shortened and pictures redrawn by Qalam", or
  nothing if unchanged. Never write "changed" for Quran text or QuranEnc translations: they must be unchanged.
- Show the line in the learner's language with the names and licence names left in Latin script, for example:
  "داستان از Afghan Children Read (Creative Associates International)، CC0، از Bloom Library."
- Some owners set exact wording; use theirs. PhET: "Simulation by PhET Interactive Simulations, University of
  Colorado Boulder, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://phet.colorado.edu)". Illustrative Mathematics: "Based on IM®
  K–12 Math authored by Illustrative Mathematics, licensed under CC BY 4.0". Tanzil: "Quran text: Tanzil Project
  (tanzil.net)". VOA: "From VOA Learning English (learningenglish.voanews.com)".
- Never use another owner's name or logo as if it endorsed us (Khan Academy, PhET, Illustrative Mathematics and
  the BBC all say so).

Each source's ready-made line is in the `attribution` field of `content/sources.json`.
