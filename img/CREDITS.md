# Picture credits

All pictures in `img/` are flat SVG, drawn for this app. No raster images, no web fonts, no external references: everything works offline.

## Scenes and app pictures

`img/topics/*.svg` (27 topic scenes), `img/app/icon.svg`, `img/app/home-children.svg`, `img/app/home-adults.svg` and `img/app/welcome.svg` were drawn new for this project and carry no third-party licence.

## Icons (`img/icons/`, 48x48, single `currentColor`)

91 icons: 55 adapted from Health Icons, 36 drawn new.

People are drawn without eyes, mouths or other facial features, in the scenes and in the icons. The only exceptions are icons where the feature is the sign itself: `eye`, `eye-blurred`, `eye-sunken` (the eye region only) and `stroke-face` (the drooping mouth only).

### Adapted from Health Icons

[Health Icons](https://healthicons.org) is made by Resolve to Save Lives for global health and is released under the MIT licence (text below). Each icon was taken from the `filled` set of the healthicons package, normalised to a 48x48 viewBox with a single `currentColor` fill and its path data rewritten in compact relative form. Any change beyond that is named.
